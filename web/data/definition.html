Genera on a rooted tree

The rule takes three inputs: a rooted tree whose leaves are the languages, a divergence for its nodes, and a threshold δ\delta.

For a node uu whose languages with data lie on at least two child branches, let Π(u)\Pi(u) be the pairs of such languages on different branches, or a uniform sample of 200 of them when there are more. Given a distance Δ\Delta between languages, the crown divergence D(u)D(u) is the mean of Δ\Delta over the pairs in Π(u)\Pi(u) for which it is defined, and uu is measured when there is at least one. A quantity of the node itself, such as its age in a dated tree, can take the place of D(u)D(u). The value on which the cut is made is

c(u)={D(u)if u is measured,max⁡{c(w):w a child of u,c(w) defined}otherwise, c(u) = \begin{cases} D(u) & \text{if } u \text{ is measured},\\ \max\{c(w) : w \text{ a child of } u,\ c(w) \text{ defined}\} & \text{otherwise,} \end{cases}

which is undefined when no node at or below uu is measured. Many nodes rest on few pairs, so a measured node is judged by its own D(u)D(u) rather than by the largest value below it, which would let a single noisy descendant force a split.

Call a node vv final at threshold δ\delta when c(v)c(v) is undefined, when c(v)≤δc(v) \le \delta, or when vv is a language. On the path v0(ℓ),…,vk(ℓ)=ℓv_0(\ell), \ldots, v_k(\ell) = \ell from the root of its tree to a language ℓ\ell, let jj be the position of the first final node, which exists because ℓ\ell itself is final. The genus of ℓ\ell is

hδ(ℓ)=vj(ℓ). h_\delta(\ell) = v_j(\ell).

It is cut by divergence when c(vj(ℓ))≤δc(v_j(\ell)) \le \delta (divergence). Otherwise it is an unmeasured clade below a node that exceeds δ\delta (unmeasured-clade), the language itself (single-language), or, when j=0j = 0, a whole tree without any measurement, kept as one genus (unmeasured-family, or isolate for a tree of one language). Writing ℒ(u)\mathcal{L}(u) for the languages at or below uu, two properties follow for any tree or forest under one condition, which holds whenever the languages are the leaves and which Glottolog 5.3 satisfies: no language lies below another language.

Proposition 1 (genera are complete clades). Every class of hδh_\delta is ℒ(u)\mathcal{L}(u) for its node uu.

Proof. Whether a node is final depends on the node alone. Let u=hδ(ℓ)u = h_\delta(\ell). Every ℓ′∈ℒ(u)\ell' \in \mathcal{L}(u) shares the nodes of ℓ\ell above uu, none of them final, and reaches uu, which is final; so hδ(ℓ′)=uh_\delta(\ell') = u. Conversely, hδ(ℓ′)=uh_\delta(\ell') = u places ℓ′\ell' at or below uu. If uu is a language, ℒ(u)={u}\mathcal{L}(u) = \{u\} by the condition. ▫\square

Proposition 2 (the partitions nest). For δ<δ′\delta < \delta', every genus at δ\delta lies inside a single genus at δ′\delta'.

Proof. Whether c(v)c(v) is defined, and whether vv is a language, do not depend on the threshold, and c(v)≤δc(v) \le \delta implies c(v)≤δ′c(v) \le \delta', so every node final at δ\delta is final at δ′\delta', and the first final node on a path at δ′\delta' lies at or above the one at δ\delta. If hδ(ℓ1)=hδ(ℓ2)=uh_\delta(\ell_1) = h_\delta(\ell_2) = u, then ℓ2∈ℒ(u)⊆ℒ(hδ′(ℓ1))\ell_2 \in \mathcal{L}(u) \subseteq \mathcal{L}(h_{\delta'}(\ell_1)), and Proposition 1 gives hδ′(ℓ2)=hδ′(ℓ1)h_{\delta'}(\ell_2) = h_{\delta'}(\ell_1). ▫\square

Every node above a genus that is not a whole tree has c>δc > \delta, and a genus cut by measured divergence has c≤δc \le \delta; the released margins are these two distances. Because a measured node is judged by its own value, a genus can still contain a measured sub-clade whose own DD exceeds δ\delta.

Word distance

Words are compared by global alignment with substitution scores for pairs of ASJP symbols learned from the data, after Jäger (2013). Same-concept words of 30,000 randomly drawn pairs of languages from the same family are aligned; pairs that align closely are taken as probable cognates; the association between the symbols aligned in them is estimated as pointwise mutual information (Church and Hanks 1990) with add-one (Laplace) smoothing, computed with the ASymCat library (Tresoldi 2026a) on FreqProb’s estimators (Tresoldi 2026b), averaged over its two directions; and the words are realigned with those scores. Three iterations are run. With s(x,y)s(x, y) the score of the best alignment of words xx and yy (Needleman–Wunsch, linear gap penalty 2.5), the word distance is

d(x,y)=1−2s(x,y)s(x,x)+s(y,y), d(x, y) = 1 - \frac{2\, s(x, y)}{s(x, x) + s(y, y)},

which is 0 for identical words and exceeds 1 when two words align worse than chance. For two languages aa and bb, let d‾ij\bar d_{ij} be the mean of dd over the forms of concept ii in aa and concept jj in bb, which averages over synonyms; let SS be the concepts attested in both, and OO a sample of 60 pairs (i,j)(i, j), i≠ji \ne j, of concepts attested in aa and bb respectively, drawn with a generator seeded by the language pair. The language distance divides the mean same-concept distance by the mean different-concept distance,

Δ(a,b)=1|S|∑i∈Sd‾ii1|O|∑(i,j)∈Od‾ij, \Delta(a, b) = \frac{\frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{i \in S} \bar d_{ii}}{\frac{1}{|O|}\sum_{(i, j) \in O} \bar d_{ij}},

defined when |S|≥20|S| \ge 20, in the way LDND normalizes edit distance (Wichmann et al. 2010). The denominator discounts the similarity that any two languages share by chance through their phonotactics.

Calibration

The threshold can be fixed or calibrated against a reference partition of some of the languages, here the WALS genera. The threshold is calibrated against the WALS genera of all families, giving each family equal weight:

A(δ)=1|F|∑f∈FARI(Wf,hδ|f),δ*=median⁡arg maxδ∈Δ141∑k=−2020A(δ+0.001k),Δ={0.520,0.521,…,0.980}, A(\delta) = \frac{1}{|F|} \sum_{f \in F} \mathrm{ARI}\left(W_f,\ h_\delta|_f\right), \qquad \delta^* = \operatorname{median}\, \operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\delta \in \Delta}\ \frac{1}{41} \sum_{k=-20}^{20} A(\delta + 0.001\,k), \qquad \Delta = \{0.520, 0.521, \ldots, 0.980\},

where FF is the set of Glottolog families whose WALS-labelled in-scope languages fall into at least two WALS genera, WfW_f the WALS genus partition of those languages, hδ|fh_\delta|_f the divergence partition restricted to them, and ARI the adjusted Rand index (Rand 1971; Hubert and Arabie 1985). The WALS label “Creoles and Pidgins” is not genealogical and is left out of calibration and evaluation alike. Averaging over families instead of pooling all languages kept the most populous WALS genera, such as Bantoid and Oceanic, from dominating the criterion. A(δ)A(\delta) is a step function that jumps whenever δ\delta crosses the value of a node in one of the families; averaging it over a window of ±0.02 makes δ*\delta^* depend on the run of the curve rather than on single crossings.
